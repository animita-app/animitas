# Cutout credits

Objects are cut out of Wikimedia Commons photos with macOS subject lifting and keep each photo's license.
The list with authors, licenses and links lives in `frontend/lib/credits.ts` and is shown in the app at `/creditos`,
which CC BY-SA requires. Add a new cutout there, never only here.

- `icons/` and `app/icon.png`: the votive candle, on paper.
- Cut-out letters are drawn live by `components/cutout-text.tsx` from the device's own typefaces.
